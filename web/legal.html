01

Terms of Service

NeoGen is an adult (18+) AI workspace and evolving Afterlife experience operated by AVDigital One through The Metasphere, a decentralized global digital ecosystem. Worldwide availability remains subject to applicable local laws, restricted territories, providers, plans, devices, and the connected Genesis runtime.

AI and approvals

Neo is AI, not a human or professional adviser. Output can be incomplete or wrong. Review and independently verify high-impact decisions. Writes, terminal commands, Git mutations, submissions, publishing, purchases, messages, and self-improvements remain bound to the exact, single-use approval shown to the user.

Your content

You keep rights you hold in uploaded content and grant the operator only the licence needed to provide, secure, maintain, and improve the requested service. You must have authority to upload and process content, repositories, data, logos, and media. Generated content may not be unique and must be checked for rights, confidentiality, accuracy, and lawful use.

Rights that cannot be waived

Nothing excludes mandatory consumer, privacy, accessibility, or other statutory rights. Liability, dispute, warranty, indemnity, and governing-law language must be localized and approved before commercial deployment; this framework does not silently invent those terms.

02

Privacy Notice

AVDigital One is the operator and controller for NeoGen account, subscription, support, security, and product data it determines how to use. NeoGen may process account and profile data, sessions, plan status, prompts and conversations, user-selected files and repository context, approvals and audit events, settings, security signals, support messages, and payment status returned by a provider. Card data must go directly to the configured payment provider.

Why and where

Processing supports the service contract, security, legal obligations, user consent where needed, and documented legitimate interests where available. User-selected prompts and content may be sent to Puter or another chosen model provider. NeoGen does not silently use private prompts or source code to train a foundation model.

Your control

Depending on location, rights may include access/know, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, consent withdrawal, portability, sale/sharing or targeted-advertising opt-out, sensitive-use limitation, non-discrimination, and regulator complaint. Requests go to the configured privacy contact.

Transfers and retention

Restricted transfers require a valid mechanism and risk assessment. The deployment must publish actual hosting regions, subprocessors, transfer safeguards, and a data-class retention schedule. No residency or deletion timing is promised unless the deployment enforces it.

03

AI Transparency

Neo identifies itself as AI and exposes the requested outcome, plan, provider boundary, data/tools in scope, permission request, execution state, verification, and recovery point. Agent Council uses ten prompted specialist calls sharing a governed transcript; Training Lab curates attributed project knowledge and tested proposals rather than silently retraining foundation-model weights.

Synthetic media must be disclosed where law or context requires. Prohibited and high-risk uses are blocked or gated by deployment-specific assessments, documentation, human oversight, logging, incident response, and sector controls.

04

Acceptable Use

Do not use NeoGen for illegal activity, exploitation of children, non-consensual intimate content, unlawful extremist activity, weapons harm, malware or credential theft, destructive intrusion, fraud, impersonation, stalking, doxxing, harassment, spam, rights infringement, unauthorized surveillance, or bypassing approval, entitlement, security, or audit controls.

Do not make unlawful solely automated high-impact decisions about employment, housing, credit, insurance, education, health, migration, policing, or essential services. Sensitive data requires authority, minimization, safeguards, and any required assessment. Authorized security testing must be proportionate and non-destructive.

05

Subscriptions & cancellation

Levels 1–10 are progressive individual plans; Admin Level 11 and Owner Level 12 are role-assigned; Business and Enterprise require organization configuration. Current paid selections create a pending request and do not activate access or charge a user until a configured provider confirms it.

Checkout must disclose price, currency, tax, interval, renewal, trial conversion, material limits, cancellation method, and local cooling-off/refund rights before payment. Cancellation must be at least as simple as sign-up where required, and non-waivable consumer remedies remain available.

06

Cookies & device storage

Necessary storage supports sessions, security, plan selection, model/API preferences, UI state, offline cache, and user-requested Puter persistence. Optional analytics, advertising, or cross-site tracking stays off until a jurisdiction-appropriate consent tool is configured. Optional consent must be specific, informed, affirmative, and as easy to reject or withdraw as to accept.

07

Accessibility

NeoGen targets WCAG 2.2 Level AA with keyboard operation, visible focus, semantic labels, contrast, responsive reflow, reduced motion, non-color status, accessible authentication, error identification, captions/transcripts where media exists, and practical touch targets. The deployed operator must publish tested conformance, known limitations, a feedback contact, and any required enforcement route.

08

Security & incidents

Controls include scoped workspaces, exact single-use approvals, credential isolation, least privilege, audit events, verification, checkpoints, recovery, secret-like file filtering, and automated tests. No system is perfectly secure. Reports go to the configured security contact; a deployment-specific security.txt must be published before launch.

Incident handling covers triage, containment, protected evidence, impact and legal assessment, eradication, recovery, applicable notifications, documentation, and corrective action. Deadlines vary by jurisdiction and incident.

09

IP & takedown

Rights holders can identify the protected work or right, challenged material, location, contact details, authority, good-faith basis, and signature. The operator may restrict material, notify the user, process a lawful counter-notice, and act against repeat infringement. US DMCA safe-harbor status is not claimed unless a designated agent is registered.

10

Regional rights

EEA/UK: GDPR/UK GDPR transparency, bases, rights, DPIAs, transfer controls, PECR/ePrivacy storage consent, and applicable EU AI Act duties. California/US states: threshold-dependent privacy notices, access/correction/deletion/portability, opt-outs, sensitive-data limits, preference signals, appeals, and COPPA controls if child-directed use ever begins.

Australia: Privacy Act, APPs, NDB scheme, and consumer guarantees. Canada: PIPEDA or provincial accountability, consent, safeguards, rights, and breach duties. Brazil: LGPD bases, rights, ANPD transfer mechanisms, and controller contact. Singapore: PDPA obligations and published DPO. New Zealand: Privacy Act principles and serious-harm notification. Japan: APPI purpose, security, rights, records, and foreign-transfer rules.

Each new market remains gated for local privacy, AI, consumer, subscriptions, tax, sanctions/export, cybersecurity, accessibility, content, gaming, and sector review.
