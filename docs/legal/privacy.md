# Privacy Notice

Version and effective date: 2026-08-05

## Controller and scope

The configured NeoGen operator is the controller for account, subscription, support, security, and product data it determines how to use. A Business or Enterprise customer may be the controller and NeoGen its processor for customer-controlled workspace data. Puter and other connected providers may act as separate controllers or processors under their own notices and contracts.

## Data categories

NeoGen may process account identifiers, contact and profile data, authentication/session records, plan and entitlement state, user prompts and conversations, files and repository metadata selected by the user, approvals and audit events, Agent Council and Training Lab records, device/network/security signals, support messages, payment status returned by a provider, and preferences stored locally or through Puter. Payment card data must go directly to a compliant payment provider and must not be stored by NeoGen.

## Purposes and legal bases

Data is used to provide requested services and contracts; authenticate and secure accounts; preserve conversations, checkpoints, approvals, and settings; process user-approved actions; meet legal obligations; prevent abuse; respond to support; and improve reliability. Where GDPR/UK GDPR applies, the operator must map each purpose to contract, legal obligation, legitimate interests documented by an assessment, consent, or another valid basis. Consent is not bundled with service access when it is not necessary.

## AI processing and human control

Prompts and selected content may be sent to Puter or another chosen provider to generate results. NeoGen exposes the provider boundary, approval scope, connected capability, and saved records. It does not silently use private prompts or source code to train a foundation model. Project-scoped strategy ranking stores outcomes rather than prompt or source content. Users can decline consequential actions and should not provide unnecessary sensitive data.

## Sharing and subprocessors

Data is disclosed only to user-selected providers, hosting/storage/security/support/payment processors, authorized organization administrators, professional advisers, or authorities when legally required. The live subprocessor list, purpose, location, transfer mechanism, and notice process must be published before Business or Enterprise processing begins.

## International transfers

Transfers must use a lawful mechanism applicable to the origin and destination, such as an adequacy decision, EU Standard Contractual Clauses, the UK IDTA/Addendum, Brazil-approved clauses, or another recognized mechanism, with transfer-risk and supplementary-measure assessments where required. Deployment configuration must identify hosting regions; this template does not promise data residency that is not technically enforced.

## Retention

The operator must publish a retention schedule by data class. Default design targets are: active account data for the account lifetime; conversations and user files until deletion or account closure; approvals/security logs only as long as needed for audit, fraud prevention, disputes, and law; failed temporary execution data for the shortest practical period; and backups on a documented rolling deletion cycle. Legal holds override deletion only where lawful.

## Rights and requests

Depending on location, people may have rights to access, know, correct, delete, restrict, object, withdraw consent, opt out of sale/sharing or targeted advertising, limit sensitive-data use, receive portable data, avoid discrimination, or complain to a regulator. Requests go to the configured privacy contact. Identity verification must be proportionate, and authorized-agent requests must be supported where required. NeoGen does not sell personal information by design; if future processing changes, the operator must add required notices and opt-outs before the change.

## Cookies and device storage

Strictly necessary session, security, preference, entitlement, and service-worker storage may be used. Non-essential analytics, advertising, or cross-site tracking must remain off until a jurisdiction-appropriate consent interface is implemented. Rejecting optional storage must be as easy as accepting it.

## Security and breaches

Controls include scoped workspaces, exact single-use approvals, credential isolation, least privilege, audit events, verification, checkpoints, recovery, secret-like file filtering, and tests. No system is perfectly secure. The operator maintains a breach-response process and will notify affected people and regulators when applicable law requires it.

## Children

NeoGen is not directed to children and requires users to be 18 or older. If the operator learns that a child's personal data was collected without valid authorization, it will disable the account and delete the data unless retention is legally required.

## Contact and changes

The deployed notice must publish the operator, privacy contact, DPO or representatives where required, and complaint route. Material changes are versioned and presented for renewed acceptance when legally necessary.
