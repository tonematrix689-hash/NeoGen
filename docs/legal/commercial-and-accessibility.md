# Subscriptions, cancellations, cookies, and accessibility

Version and effective date: 2026-08-05

## Subscription disclosures

Before payment, show the selected level, included and locked abilities, total price and currency, taxes, billing interval, renewal date, trial conversion, minimum term, cancellation steps, material limits, payment provider, and refund/cooling-off rights. Do not use preselected paid options, disguised advertisements, false scarcity, or a harder cancellation path. Provide an immediate receipt and renewal/cancellation confirmation.

The current code records paid-plan requests as `pending_provider`; it does not claim payment or activation. Admin Level 11 and Owner Level 12 are role-assigned. Business and Enterprise activation requires provider and organization configuration.

## Refunds and statutory rights

The deployed refund policy must preserve all non-waivable local rights, including applicable cooling-off, digital-content, defective-service, misleading-conduct, and charge-dispute remedies. Operator-specific refund periods and exceptions must not be invented until the operator and payment provider are configured.

## Cookies and local storage

Necessary storage covers authentication/session state, plan selection, model preference, API endpoint, UI preference, service-worker cache, and user-requested Puter persistence. Optional analytics, advertising, personalization across services, or marketing storage must be separately categorized, disabled before consent where required, and revocable through Settings.

## Accessibility

NeoGen targets WCAG 2.2 Level AA: keyboard access, visible focus, semantic landmarks and labels, sufficient contrast, responsive reflow, reduced-motion support, non-color status cues, accessible authentication, error identification, captions/transcripts where media exists, and touch-target sizing. The deployed Accessibility Statement must name the tested version, known limitations, feedback contact, response path, and any legally required enforcement body.
