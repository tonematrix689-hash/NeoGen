# International Launch Checklist

Version: 2026-08-05.1

## Blocking operator configuration

- [x] Operator name disclosed as AVDigital One
- [x] Product and ecosystem relationship disclosed: NeoGen operates through The Metasphere
- [ ] Exact legal form, registration number, jurisdiction, and registered/service address
- [ ] Governing law and dispute terms approved for the first launch market
- [ ] Support, privacy, security, and legal contacts
- [ ] DPO, EU representative, UK representative, privacy officer, or local agent where required
- [ ] Actual hosting locations, data flows, retention schedule, backup deletion, and transfer mechanisms
- [ ] Puter and every live subprocessor classified and contractually reviewed
- [ ] Payment provider, merchant of record, prices, currencies, taxes, refund/cooling-off terms, cancellation flow, and receipts
- [ ] Product insurance and incident contacts appropriate to operations

## Product controls

- [ ] Current mandatory-document versions displayed before account use and exact acceptance recorded
- [ ] Optional cookies off until valid consent; withdrawal and preference signal handling tested
- [ ] Export, access, correction, deletion, portability, objection/opt-out, and complaint workflow tested
- [ ] Account deletion propagates to live systems and documented backup cycle
- [ ] Adult eligibility rule enforced without unnecessary date-of-birth collection
- [ ] AI identity, provider, limitations, synthetic-media disclosure, and human approval are visible
- [ ] High-risk/prohibited AI uses blocked or supported by required assessments and controls
- [ ] Accessibility audit against WCAG 2.2 AA and regional standards completed
- [ ] Security review, dependency scan, secrets scan, restore test, breach tabletop, and `security.txt` complete
- [ ] Copyright/takedown, repeat-infringer, law-enforcement request, and appeals procedures staffed

## Market-by-market gate

- [ ] Privacy, AI, consumer, subscription, tax, export/sanctions, cybersecurity, accessibility, online-safety, gaming, and sector rules assessed
- [ ] Local language, notices, consent age, cancellation, regulator contacts, and mandatory terms added
- [ ] Marketing claims substantiated; no claim that NeoGen is universally compliant or that AI output is professional advice
- [ ] Qualified local counsel signs off before paid public launch
