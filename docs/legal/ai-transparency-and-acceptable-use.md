# AI Transparency and Acceptable Use

Version and effective date: 2026-08-05

## Transparency

Neo is an AI assistant, not a human. Puter or another selected provider generates model output. Ten Agent Council roles are ten prompted specialist calls sharing a governed transcript; they are not ten independent employees or authorities. Training Lab stores source-attributed project knowledge and tested improvement proposals; it does not silently retrain foundation-model weights.

For each consequential workflow NeoGen should show: the requested outcome, planned steps, data and tools in scope, provider/connection boundary, requested permission, execution state, result, verification, and recovery point. Synthetic media should carry a clear disclosure where law or context requires it. High-risk or prohibited uses must be disabled, and deployers remain responsible for sector-specific assessments.

## Acceptable use

Users must not use NeoGen to:

- violate law, sanctions, export controls, court orders, or third-party rights;
- exploit, groom, sexualize, endanger, or identify children;
- create non-consensual intimate imagery, sexual violence, or unlawful extremist content;
- facilitate weapons, malware, credential theft, destructive intrusion, evasion, fraud, impersonation, stalking, doxxing, or harassment;
- make unlawful or solely automated high-impact decisions about employment, housing, credit, insurance, education, health, migration, policing, or essential services;
- process biometric, health, financial, precise-location, protected-class, or other sensitive data without authority, safeguards, and required assessments;
- scrape or monitor people unlawfully, bypass robots/access controls, or send spam and deceptive communications;
- infringe copyright, trademarks, privacy, publicity, confidentiality, database, or contractual rights;
- bypass NeoGen approvals, forge audit records, self-grant Admin/Owner roles, or conceal a consequential action.

Security research is permitted only on systems the user owns or is expressly authorized to test and must remain proportionate and non-destructive.

## Enforcement and appeals

The operator may warn, restrict, suspend, or terminate access proportionately to risk and legal duties, preserve necessary evidence, and report imminent or legally reportable threats. Where appropriate, users receive the reason and a route to appeal through the configured support contact.
