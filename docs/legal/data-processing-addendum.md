# Data Processing Addendum and Subprocessor Framework

Version: 2026-08-05.1
Effective date: 2026-08-05

This framework applies when a Business or Enterprise customer controls personal data processed by NeoGen on its behalf. A signed order form must identify the parties and prevail for commercial details.

## Processing instructions

NeoGen processes customer personal data only on documented instructions, including service configuration and user-approved actions, unless law requires otherwise. The customer is responsible for lawful instructions, notices, bases, user permissions, and data minimization.

## Required contract schedule

Before execution, complete: subject matter and duration; processing nature and purpose; data-subject categories; personal-data categories; sensitive-data restrictions; permitted regions; retention/deletion; security measures; approved subprocessors; transfer mechanism; audit method; and contacts.

## Processor duties

NeoGen will bind authorized personnel to confidentiality; implement proportionate technical and organizational measures; assist with rights requests, assessments, consultations, and breaches; delete or return data at termination subject to law; make compliance information available; notify the customer if an instruction appears unlawful; and permit proportionate audits under agreed confidentiality, security, frequency, and cost rules.

## Subprocessors

Subprocessors must be bound to equivalent privacy and security duties. The live register must include legal entity, service, data categories, processing locations, and transfer mechanism. Customers receive reasonable notice of new subprocessors and a documented objection path. Puter and the final hosting, email, support, analytics, security, and payment vendors cannot be listed until their actual deployed roles are confirmed.

## Transfers

Where required, attach the current EU controller/processor clauses, EU transfer SCC module, UK IDTA or Addendum, Brazil standard clauses, or another lawful mechanism without altering mandatory text. Complete transfer-impact and supplementary-measures assessments before restricted transfers.

## Security schedule baseline

Least privilege; scoped repositories; exact, single-use approvals; encryption in transit; protected credential handling; provider isolation; logging and monitoring; tested backups/checkpoints; secure development and dependency review; vulnerability and incident management; deletion controls; business continuity; and periodic access review. Encryption at rest, regional residency, recovery objectives, certifications, and penetration-test commitments must reflect the actual deployment rather than this template.
