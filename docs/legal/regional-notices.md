# Regional Privacy and Consumer Notices

Version and effective date: 2026-08-05

## European Economic Area

Publish the controller, DPO/representative where required, purposes, Article 6 bases, Article 9 conditions, recipients, transfers, retention, rights, complaint authority, mandatory/optional fields, automated-decision details, and source of indirectly collected data. Conduct DPIAs for likely high-risk processing. Meet applicable EU AI Act provider/deployer, transparency, literacy, technical documentation, risk, logging, human-oversight, incident, and prohibited-practice duties before offering a covered use.

## United Kingdom

Apply UK GDPR and Data Protection Act duties, PECR storage/direct-marketing consent, appropriate UK transfer mechanisms, and current ICO international-transfer guidance. Identify a UK representative where required.

## California and other US states

If threshold and scope tests apply, disclose categories collected, sources, purposes, recipients, retention, sale/sharing status, sensitive-data uses, and request methods. Support access/know, correction, deletion, portability, opt-out, limit, non-discrimination, authorized agents, and recognized preference signals where required. State-specific notices and appeal routes must be added after deployment reach and data practices are known. COPPA controls apply if the service becomes directed to children under 13 or gains actual knowledge of their collection; the current adult-only rule avoids knowingly offering that service.

## Australia

Apply the Privacy Act and Australian Privacy Principles where covered, including transparent management, collection notices, use/disclosure, overseas disclosure, security, access/correction, direct marketing, and the Notifiable Data Breaches scheme. Consumer guarantees and misleading-conduct rules remain non-waivable.

## Canada

Where PIPEDA or substantially similar provincial laws apply, implement accountable privacy management, identified purposes, meaningful consent, limiting collection/use/disclosure/retention, safeguards, openness, access/correction, complaint handling, and breach reporting/recordkeeping. Quebec and provincial requirements need a deployment-specific supplement.

## Brazil

Where LGPD applies, identify the controller and contact, purposes and bases, rights route, sharing, retention, security, and international-transfer mechanism. Use ANPD-approved transfer mechanisms and clauses where required.

## Singapore

Where the PDPA applies, designate and publish a DPO contact and implement accountability, notification, consent, purpose limitation, accuracy, protection, retention limitation, transfer limitation, access/correction, breach notification, and applicable do-not-call duties.

## New Zealand

Apply the Privacy Act 2020 information privacy principles, overseas-disclosure requirements, access/correction, and serious-harm breach notification. Publish a privacy officer contact.

## Japan

Where APPI applies, publish use purposes, security and complaint information; support disclosure/correction/cessation rights; maintain transfer records; and use consent, adequacy, or recipient safeguards for foreign transfers as required.

## Other locations

Country launch remains gated until counsel confirms privacy, AI, consumer, subscription, tax, sanctions/export, cybersecurity, accessibility, online-safety, content, employment, gaming, and sector-specific obligations. Geolocation must not be used to make legal assumptions without a documented basis and user notice.
