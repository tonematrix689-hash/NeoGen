# Milestone 017 — Decision Symbiosis

NeoGen's first symbiosis milestone is a consented personal decision memory—not a biological implant. VERA can preserve explicit decisions, their context, provenance, confidence, later outcomes, and revocation state. Records remain scoped to their owner.

VERA adapts its autonomy deterministically:

- **Observe:** collect evidence without changing the user's world.
- **Advise:** recommend an option while the user acts.
- **Prepare:** construct an exact action and request approval once.
- **Human only:** block autonomous execution for high-impact actions.

Risk increases when an action affects other people, processes sensitive information, or is difficult to reverse. Medical, legal, financial, identity, permission, deployment, and physical-world actions retain explicit human control. Outcomes improve future recommendations but never silently rewrite a user's values or permissions.

Nanite-based biological symbiosis remains a speculative research horizon. NeoGen must not represent it as safe, available, therapeutic, or clinically validated without appropriate scientific evidence, ethics oversight, regulation, and informed consent.
