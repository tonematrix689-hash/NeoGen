# Milestone 016 — Coins of the Dead Avatar Forge

NeoGen now includes a development-only closed-loop currency ledger named Coins of the Dead (COTD) and an AI-assisted layered avatar forge.

## Product contract

- The fixed design reference is 100 COTD to AUD 1.00. It is a non-executable quote, not a live exchange promise.
- Real-money purchase, cash redemption, peer transfer, custody, and blockchain settlement remain disabled.
- Every COTD debit is ledgered. A layer mint or rarity upgrade requires one exact, single-use user approval.
- AI creates an editable design proposal; it cannot spend, mint, or upgrade without that approval.
- Forge avatars support 1,000 rarity levels and staged bones, muscle, skin, armor, weapons, accessories, and free-form appendages.
- Powers unlock for later design at milestone rarity levels. Arbitrary anatomy is represented as a composable blueprint rather than a fixed humanoid model.

## Future boundary

Payment, identity, custody, redemption, marketplace settlement, and optional on-chain publication must be separate adapters. They require production security review and applicable Australian legal and regulatory review before activation. The current system must not describe COTD as decentralized while issuance and trading remain controlled by NeoGen.
