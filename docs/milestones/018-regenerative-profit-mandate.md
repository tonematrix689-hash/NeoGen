# Milestone 018 — Regenerative Profit Mandate

The owner directs 100% of **distributable profit**—only after taxes, wages, refunds, operating obligations, and required reserves—into four equal allocations: renewable energy, environmental restoration, sustainable and diverse agriculture, and ethical precious metals and gems.

NeoGen calculates non-executable proposals and preserves every cent when division produces a remainder. It does not transfer or invest money. Every proposal requires owner review plus applicable director, accounting, tax, legal, and licensed financial review.

Metals and gems require verified ethical provenance, documented chain of custody, counterparty checks, investment-grade specifications, and independent audit or assurance. Recycled material must be separately identified. Mined material is eligible only when traceable, responsibly sourced, and supported by risk-based due diligence.
