## Required before public commerce

The software must not infer or invent the operating entity's legal details. Before a
public paid launch, configure the operator name and address, privacy and legal contacts,
governing law and venue, processor/subprocessor list, retention schedule, transfer
mechanism, payment provider and refund rules, tax handling, supported territories, and
breach-response ownership. Obtain qualified local counsel review for each launch market.

This checkpoint is an implementation baseline and audit record, not a legal opinion or a
claim that one static policy guarantees compliance in every jurisdiction.
